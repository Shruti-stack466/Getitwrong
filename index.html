Evidence checklist

Lab rules and other ways to do this lab

What help is allowed

  • Use only the institution-approved AI tool and permitted account.
  • Write your own revised prompt, check and conclusion.
  • Do not use another learner’s analysis as yours.

Use fictional data only

  • Do not enter passwords, Aadhaar numbers, contact details, health information, marks or confidential records.
  • Tell the facilitator if real private information appears.

Slow internet

  • Record the answer as text instead of a screenshot.
  • Send one prompt. Wait for the full answer. Then record it.

Sharing one device

  • Take turns on the AI tool.
  • Sign out after your attempts.
  • Everyone keeps their own record. Do not type in someone else’s.

Cannot sign in or get access

  • Ask your facilitator to check your account. Do not use a friend’s account or password.
  • If access still fails, use a facilitator-approved printed or pre-captured response.
