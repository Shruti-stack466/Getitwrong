01 / 05

Short Instructions

What Help Is Allowed

Use only the institution-approved AI tool and permitted account. Write your own revised prompt, check and conclusion. Do not use another learner’s analysis as yours.

Keep the Task Moving

On a shared device, take turns and sign out after your attempts. With low bandwidth, record text instead of a screenshot. If access fails, use a facilitator-approved printed or pre-captured response.

Use Fictional Data Only

Do not enter passwords, Aadhaar numbers, contact details, health information, marks or confidential records. Tell the facilitator if real private information appears.
